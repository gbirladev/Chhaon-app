Staying informed without making a call

Alerts · live

A missed dose, no reply to the check-in, a call request, low stock or an appointment: in the app and, if you agree, on WhatsApp.

Notifications · live

A list of what happened, newest first, with unread dots. Swipe a row to delete it.

Message history · live

A screen showing what was really sent to the parent and how they answered, so nothing is a mystery.

Appointments · live

Add a doctor visit. The parent gets a WhatsApp reminder before it, and the family sees it on Today.

Daily check-in · live

A good-morning message with one tap: I’m okay, or Call me. No answer in a few hours alerts the family.

Time zones · live

Reminders follow the parent’s clock, so 8 AM is 8 AM for them. The caregiver sees both times side by side.
