The family together, and ready for emergencies

Family circle · live

Invite brothers and sisters with a link. They share one parent profile, and the owner can remove members.

Several parents · live

One account can look after more than one parent, each with their own medicines, language and time zone, within the plan.

Shared phone · live

Two parents may use one WhatsApp number. Each gets their own reminder, and STOP applies to the phone.

Emergency card · live

A link with medicines, allergies, blood group and contacts that opens without a login. It can be revoked at any time.

Reports wallet · live

Keep prescriptions and lab reports as photos or PDFs for each parent, in private storage, with limits by plan.

Your own profile · built, off

Add yourself and get reminders for your own medicines on your own WhatsApp, private from the rest of the family.

Live = in use now · Built, off = finished and tested, switched on later
