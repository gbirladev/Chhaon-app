The safety net and the hearts: built, switched on after testing

Safety net

Nudge the parent first, then decide. The family is alerted after a pattern of misses, not for one forgotten tap.

Critical medicines

Mark a medicine such as insulin as critical, and the family is alerted on the very first miss.

Silent-parent pause

If a parent says nothing for days, nudges and alerts pause and the family gets one clear summary.

Hearts

Tap a heart or write up to 150 characters. The parent sees “Riya saw that you took your medicine ❤️”.

Replies to hearts

The parent’s emoji or words appear next to your heart, with whether it was delivered or read.

Gentle limits

Hearts wait for the morning, go at most three a day, and the parent can reply NO HEARTS to stop only them.

All six are finished and tested. They are switched on per family after real-phone checks.
