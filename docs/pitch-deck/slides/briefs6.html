The platform: accounts, plans, data and feedback

Sign-up and profile · live

Sign up with your email. A parent’s profile holds age, city, conditions, allergies and blood group.

Plans and trial · live

Free, Plus, Family and Ultra, with a 30-day trial. Reminders above a plan limit pause with a clear warning, never deleted.

Your data · live

Download everything or delete the account at any time. A single parent profile can be deleted too.

Installable app · live

Add it to the home screen like an app, with pull to refresh and a short guide for Android and iPhone.

Feedback · live

Send an idea or a problem, with up to three screenshots, from inside the app.

Report reading · built, off

AI reads a lab report and explains it in simple English or Hindi. It never diagnoses and always says to ask a doctor.

Live = in use now · Built, off = finished and tested, switched on later
