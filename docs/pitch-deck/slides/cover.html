Chhaon

छाँव

Know your parents took their medicine, without making the call.

chhaon.site/home · English and Hindi

Chhaon (छाँव) means shade or shelter. It is a family health and care app for adult children who live away from their parents, in another city in India or abroad. The parent installs nothing: they only use WhatsApp. Say who you are and that this is a pilot-stage product.
