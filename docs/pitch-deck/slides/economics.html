Cheap by design. Reminders are short utility messages, and messages inside the 24-hour window after a parent replies cost nothing extra.

Costs are capped. Plan limits and a hidden ceiling stop one account from costing without bound.

We keep it honest. Promotional-type messages cost more, so they are used sparingly.
