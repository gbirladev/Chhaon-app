Every account starts with a 30-day trial at the Plus limits. Nothing is charged yet. Prices to test in the pilot: about ₹199 to 299 a month in India, and $5 to 8 a month for families abroad. Payments by UPI (Razorpay) and international cards come after the pilot.

The plan limits are built and enforced in the product; reminders above a plan’s limit are paused with a clear warning, never deleted. The prices are experiments to validate with pilot families, not final. A strong signal is 5 to 10 pilot families who agree to pay.
