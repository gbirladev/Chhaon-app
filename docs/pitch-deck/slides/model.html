Every account starts with a 30-day trial at the Plus limits. Nothing is charged yet. Payments by UPI (Razorpay) and international cards come after the pilot.

Price to test

₹199–299 a month in India, $5–8 for families abroad

Cost to serve (estimate)

₹10–40 per parent per month for WhatsApp messages

Prices are experiments for the pilot. The cost is an estimate from third-party price lists, to be checked against Meta’s rate card.

14

The plan limits are built and enforced in the product; reminders above a plan’s limit are paused with a clear warning, never deleted. The prices are experiments to validate with pilot families, not final. Set against them, the cost to serve is about ₹10 to ₹40 per parent per month for WhatsApp messages (see the unit-economics slide for the working), so the margin is wide, but it depends on Meta’s rates and on how many messages are billed as marketing. A strong signal is 5 to 10 pilot families who agree to pay.
