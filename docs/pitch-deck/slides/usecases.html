Use cases

One simple idea, three real situations

A parent in another city

Papa in Lucknow takes his BP and sugar tablets. His son in Bengaluru sees each dose on his phone and is alerted if one is missed.

A mother-to-be

A long course of supplements and doctor-advised medicines through pregnancy and after the birth. She gets gentle WhatsApp reminders, and her husband or mother knows she is on track.

Your own medicines

A busy caregiver who also takes a daily tablet adds themselves and gets reminders on their own WhatsApp, private from the rest of the family.

Chhaon reminds and keeps the family informed. The medicines always come from the person’s doctor.
