**Full description**
```
Living away from your parents? Chhaon (छाँव — "shade") helps you look after them every day, from any city or country.

The best part: your parents don't need to install anything. No app, no account, no new habits. They simply get friendly WhatsApp messages — a medicine reminder with a photo of the medicine and a one-tap "✅ Taken" button, and a warm good-morning check-in. You see everything in the Chhaon app.

FOR YOUR PARENTS (on WhatsApp only)
• Medicine reminders at the right time, in English or Hindi, with the medicine's photo
• One tap to confirm — or just reply with a 👍
• A daily "Namaste 🙏 how are you today?" check-in
• Short, respectful messages designed for elders

FOR YOU (in the app)
• Today dashboard: every dose taken, missed or pending — at a glance
• Instant alert if a dose is missed or the morning check-in gets no reply
• Medicine schedules with photos, stock counts and refill alerts
• Appointments with WhatsApp reminders
• Health wallet: keep prescriptions and reports safely in one place
• Family circle: siblings and relatives share the same parent's care
• Emergency card: a link with medicines, allergies and blood group that works without any login — for neighbours, drivers or doctors
• Works in English and Hindi, and across time zones — reminders always follow your parent's clock

MADE FOR INDIAN FAMILIES
Chhaon is built in India for families spread across India and the world — children in the US, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia or another Indian city caring for parents back home.

PRIVATE AND SECURE
Your family's health data is encrypted, stored securely, and never sold. Your parent's consent is taken on WhatsApp before any message is sent, and they can stop messages anytime by replying STOP. You can export your data or delete your account whenever you want. Privacy policy: https://chhaon.site/legal/privacy.html

Chhaon is a care-coordination app, not a medical device. It does not give medical advice — always consult your doctor.

Start today: add your parent in two minutes, and tonight their first reminder arrives on WhatsApp. 🌿
```
