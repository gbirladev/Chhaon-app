Vatsalaya

Look after your parents' health from anywhere

For families whose parents live in another city or another country.

What we do

Vatsalaya helps adult children keep track of an ageing parent's medicines, daily well-being and doctor visits. You use our web app. Your parent only receives WhatsApp messages, so they never have to install anything or create an account.

  • Medicine reminders on WhatsApp, with a one-tap "Taken" button.
  • A short daily "are you okay?" message.
  • Appointment reminders.
  • An alert to you if a dose is missed or a check-in gets no reply.
  • Family sharing for siblings, and an emergency card you can share by link.

Messages are sent in English or Hindi, in the parent's own time zone.

Your parent decides

Before we send anything, your parent is asked on WhatsApp whether they agree. If they say no, or reply STOP at any time, we stop messaging them.

Not medical advice. Vatsalaya does not give medical advice, does not diagnose and never tells anyone to change a medicine. Please consult a doctor for any health question.
