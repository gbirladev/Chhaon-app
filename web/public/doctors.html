Help your patients take the medicines you prescribe

Between visits you can't see whether a prescription is being taken. Chhaon sends a WhatsApp reminder at each dose time, to your patient or to whoever gives the medicine at home, tells the family when a dose gets no reply, and gives you a one-page summary at the next visit. It is free, and there is nothing extra for you to do.

Families use it for an older parent (the main use), during a pregnancy, and for a baby or young child.

How Chhaon helps you and your patients

For your patient's care

  • See what happened between visits. At the next visit you get one page: the medicines you prescribed and how many doses the patient confirmed in the last 30 days, medicine by medicine. You can see which medicine is being missed, not just that something is.
  • A reminder at every dose time. A short WhatsApp message in Hindi or English with the medicine, the dose and how the tablet looks ("🔵 Blue round tablet"). The patient taps ✅ Taken. No app and no account: it works on any phone with WhatsApp.
  • The right person is reminded. If an attendant, a maid or a family member gives the medicine, the reminder can go to them instead, naming the patient. A child's reminders always go to the adult who gives the medicine.
  • A family that knows the plan. A son or daughter in another city sees what you prescribed and is told when a dose gets no reply, so the plan does not depend on one phone call.
  • A clear record in an emergency. The family keeps an emergency card with the medicines, allergies, conditions, blood group and contacts, and can share it with one link. Anyone treating your patient reads the same list you prescribed.
  • Prescriptions and reports in one place. The family keeps them as photos or PDFs, so your patient arrives with the paperwork instead of a memory of it.

For your practice

  • A better consultation. The family arrives with the medicine list and the last 30 days, instead of "I think he took them". Less of the visit goes on finding out what happened.
  • Continuity. The next visit starts from what you prescribed and what was actually taken.
  • Patients are reminded of your appointments. If the family adds the visit, the patient is reminded the evening before and the morning of it.
  • Nothing to set up and nothing to pay. No login, no app, no integration, no data sent to you. At the visit, just ask: "Do you have the Chhaon summary?" (In the app: Family, then Share with doctor.)
  • A say in what we build. We are new and building with doctors. Tell us what would make Chhaon more useful for your clinic and we read every message.

These benefits come from the product itself. Chhaon does not offer doctors anything for recommending it, because the only good reason to mention it is that it helps your patient.

If there is no reply, the family is told, in the app and optionally on WhatsApp. If the patient taps Call me, the family is told to call. The patient is also reminded the evening before and the morning of a doctor visit. Nothing is sent until the patient agrees.

What the family gets

  • Today screen: which doses were taken, missed or still pending, and whether the daily check-in was answered.
  • Alerts in the app, and optionally on WhatsApp, when a dose goes unconfirmed or the check-in gets no reply.
  • The medicine list: name, dose, exact times, before or after food, and the stock left, with a warning before it runs out.
  • Doctor visits: the next appointment, with WhatsApp reminders to the patient.
  • Prescriptions and reports kept in one place, as a photo or PDF.
  • Emergency card: medicines, allergies, conditions, blood group and contacts on one page, shared by a link the family can switch off at any time.
  • Family sharing: brothers and sisters follow the same person, see who gave which dose, and can mark a dose given in person.
  • A weekly summary on WhatsApp every Sunday: doses confirmed, any missed, refills due and the next visit.
  • Pregnancy and child care: the pregnancy week, and a suggested vaccination plan for a child, both shown as suggestions to confirm with the doctor.

How a patient starts

  1. The family scans the QR card at your desk (usually a son or daughter, often in another city).
  2. They add the parent and the medicines you prescribed.
  3. The parent, or whoever gives the medicine, gets a short WhatsApp message at each dose time and taps ✅ Taken. If no reply comes, the family is told.

Questions doctors ask

Does Chhaon change or suggest medicines?

No. The family enters the medicines from your prescription and Chhaon only reminds. It never suggests, changes or diagnoses anything. The list is only as accurate as what the family typed, so it is worth a quick look at it during the visit.

What does "not confirmed" mean?

No one tapped ✅ Taken on that reminder (the patient, or whoever gives the medicine). The dose may still have been taken. Doses that were paused, or are still waiting, are not counted.

Can the patient say no, or stop?

Yes. Nothing is sent until the patient agrees on WhatsApp, and replying STOP at any time ends all messages.

Does the patient need a smartphone?

The patient needs WhatsApp, so a smartphone. Chhaon cannot reach a patient who does not use WhatsApp.

Which medicines does it work for?

It is built for regular medicines taken at fixed times, such as for blood pressure or diabetes. It is not built for medicines taken only when needed.

What data is kept, and who can see it?

The details the family adds (the patient's name, age, city, conditions, allergies and medicines) and whether each dose was confirmed on WhatsApp. Only the family members the account holder invites can see it. We do not sell data and we show no advertisements. You see nothing unless the family shows you the summary. Read the privacy policy.

Is it an emergency service?

No. In an emergency, call 112. Chhaon's alerts depend on the patient's reply and can be late.

What does it cost?

It is free for doctors. Chhaon is in its pilot and families are not charged. If that ever changes, they will see the price before they pay.

Pilot version: for now a family can add up to 4 parents, 10 people in the family group, 20 medicines for each parent and 100 reports for each parent. These details may change after the pilot.

Will you contact my patients or promote my clinic?

No. We do not contact your patients, share their details with you, or promote clinics. Chhaon gives you nothing in return for recommending it.
