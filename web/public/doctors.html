Chhaon

Help your patients take the medicines you prescribe

Chhaon sends free WhatsApp reminders to older patients and tells their family if a dose is missed. The patient installs nothing.

How it works for your patient

  1. The family scans the QR card at your desk (usually a son or daughter, often in another city).
  2. They add the parent and the medicines you prescribed.
  3. The parent gets a short WhatsApp message at each dose time and taps ✅ Taken. If no reply comes, the family is told.

What it means for you

  • Patients who actually take what you prescribe, between visits.
  • A family that knows the plan, even when they live far away.
  • Coming later, only with the family's consent: a short summary of missed doses before each visit.

Free, and nothing in return

Chhaon is free for you and your patients. We do not pay doctors, give gifts, or promote clinics in return for recommending Chhaon, in line with the Medical Council's code of ethics on referrals (Regulation 6.4). Recommend it only if you think it helps your patients.

Chhaon never asks patients for money, OTPs, Aadhaar or bank details.
