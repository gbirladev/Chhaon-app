Privacy policy

Version: 2026-10. The in-app policy is the same text in English and Hindi.

What Vatsalaya does

Vatsalaya helps you look after a parent who lives far away. You use our web app. Your parent only gets WhatsApp messages: medicine reminders, a daily "are you okay?" and appointment reminders. They never need to install anything.

What we keep

  • About you: your name, phone number or email, and your language.
  • About your parent: name, age, city, WhatsApp number, time zone, health conditions, allergies, blood group and emergency contacts that you add.
  • Medicines and doctor visits you add, and whether each dose was confirmed on WhatsApp.
  • Photos of medicines and, if report reading is switched on, reports you upload.

Why we keep it

Only to run the service: to send reminders at the right time, to show you what happened today, and to alert you when something needs attention. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertisements.

Who can see it

  • You, and the family members you invite to the same parent. They can see that parent's details.
  • Anyone you give the emergency link to. It shows only the basic emergency details and you can turn it off at any time.
  • The services we use to run Vatsalaya: Supabase (database and storage), WhatsApp / Meta (to send messages to your parent and to you), and an AI service only if you use report reading.

Your parent's consent

Before we send anything, your parent is asked on WhatsApp to agree. If they say no, or reply STOP at any time, we stop messaging them.

Your choices

  • Download all your data from Privacy & data in the app.
  • Delete your account from Privacy & data. If you are the only person looking after a parent, that parent's data is deleted too.
  • Turn off WhatsApp alerts to you, or reply STOP to our message.

Safety

Files are kept in private storage and every account can only see its own family's data. No system is perfectly safe, but we protect your data with care.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will ask you to agree again in the app.
